# 0001 Product positioning and first audience

- **Status:** Accepted for landing-page launch
- **Date:** 2026-10-06

## Decision

Position MatterZero as applicant readiness automation for immigration teams. Lead the website with Indian immigration consultancies and include U.S. immigration law firms as a secondary audience. The first deep product example remains U.S. O-1A pre-matter preparation.

## Why

The core workflow is cross-market: collect applicant facts, organize documents and evidence, identify discrepancies, and hand the file to a human reviewer. Indian consultancies offer a clear initial buyer and local pricing context. O-1A is a rich portfolio workflow for demonstrating source-linked evidence and reviewer controls.

## Guardrails

- Do not market as a replacement CRM or a visa-eligibility AI.
- Do not imply the O-1A demo represents full support for every Indian or U.S. immigration pathway.
- Keep product and legal review under human control.

## Revisit when

Customer interviews identify a stronger primary segment, or pilot data shows materially different workflows and willingness to pay.
