# Pricing research and launch recommendation

- **Research date:** 2026-10-06
- **Recommendation:** do not publish a fixed rate until the first pilot interviews measure usage and value.

## Interpretation

The previous ₹9,999 / ₹24,999 / ₹49,999 monthly tiers were not validated. The market references show that a consultancy can buy a broad India-focused CRM at a few thousand rupees per month, while U.S. legal platforms commonly use substantially higher per-user rates. MatterZero is narrower than a full CRM but has variable voice and document-processing costs, so neither competitor price is a direct price recommendation.

## Launch approach

Use a limited founding-team pilot with a clear case/usage allowance and direct onboarding. Discuss pilot pricing after discovery, then measure active cases, staff time saved, document rework, support load, and variable provider costs. Publish tiers only after repeated evidence supports them. On the public site, say pilot pricing is shaped around team size and workflow; do not show invented “starting at” amounts.
